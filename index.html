ZX ROM Extractor

ZRE is a ZX Spectrum program that copies out the SOS or TR-DOS ROM without a programmer or even unscrewing the case, as a screen dump, a tape file, or a set of QR codes.

Download TAP

Load the tape on your ZX Spectrum, select which ROM to extract (SOS or TR-DOS), and press 3 to generate a set of QR codes. Each code takes about a minute. There are 6 codes in total. Then insert the QR code images in the form below and press Decode.

You can also extract the ROM as a hex dump on the screen (but you'll need to decode it yourself) or save it to the tape.

Join QR photos into a ROM

Add the JPEG or PNG photos. They are read in alphabetical order and saved as one binary file.
